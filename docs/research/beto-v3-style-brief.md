# Blind TRAIN style edit, protocol v1

This is one editorial revision of300 existing original synthetic TRAIN reviews, not new extra samples. It was preregistered at d0bde7e50dc7a101cd696a67f8fe0979bd881397 before editing or sentiment model training. Read only this brief and your assigned packet. Intended labels, author rationales, other batches, previous reviews and classifier results are withheld. Do not open them.

For each text, preserve every material fact and its global subjective stance, including the logical scope of negation, ironic meaning and resolution of incidents. Do not change an opinion into a factual text or vice versa. Keep natural Spanish/regional wording. Individually rewrite syntax and punctuation to its assigned style without stock templates, filler, emotion-word swaps, mechanical punctuation deletion or copying chunks across texts. The profiles are counterbalanced across intended classes by the coordinator; do not guess or target a sentiment label.

Profiles: fluido = connected clauses and natural paragraphs, roughly1 sentence for short,2–3 for medium,5–6 for long, commas where natural. Fragmentado = short complete sentences, roughly2–3 for short,5–6 for medium,10–12 for long, fewer comma chains. Mixto = balanced reporting/narration, roughly2 sentences short,4 medium,7–9 long. These sentence numbers are editorial guides, not permission for awkward or ungrammatical prose. Keep meaningful vocabulary varied; do not systematically prefer praise/complaint formulas.

Strict original word bounds: short15–30,medium50–85,long140–180 using len(text.split()). Target approximately22/65/160, while preserving content and naturalness. No artificial padding. Existing lengthy descriptions contain enough factual detail; restructure them. New text should differ substantively in syntax from original while keeping meaning. Do not edit or inspect any non-TRAIN corpus.

Write a separate JSON {actual_task: YOUR_CANONICAL_TASK, protocol:'beto-v3-training-style-revision-v1', human:false, revisions:[{id: ORIGINAL_ID, text: EDITED_TEXT, profile: ASSIGNED_PROFILE, editing_notes: concise note}]}. Exactly60 IDs, each once. Preserve profile. Only your assigned output file under artifacts/v3-style, no source edits or commits. Scripts may assemble metadata/validate words and coverage but not generate/rewrite text with templates. Every edit must be individually composed. Report completed counts/bounds; do not invent audit or agreement results.
